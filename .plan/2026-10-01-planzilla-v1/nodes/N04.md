Graph table back without touching any other byte, parse a node's brief into fields and criteria, and parse
`.plan/config.md` with defaults. No subprocess, no git, no printing.

file(s), its log, `runs/<id>/` per §7/§10); `config.py` - `load_config(repo)` returning defaults when the file is
